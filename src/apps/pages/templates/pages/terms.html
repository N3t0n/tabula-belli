Terms of Use

Last updated: 8 October 2026

1. Using the published demo

Tabula Belli lets you explore tabletop event discovery, organisation and registration. This public installation is an interactive portfolio demonstration. Its initial events and player profiles are fictional, and listings and registrations are for testing, not real bookings or commitments to attend.

These terms describe the conditions for using the demonstration. Please read them before creating an account or using the shared demo account. Personal data is handled as described in the Privacy Policy.

2. Your account and the shared demo

You can create your own account with an email address you can access and verify. Real names and a personal address are not required. Keep your own login credentials private and use only an email address you control.

The login page also provides credentials for a shared demo account. All visitors using it share the same identity and event data, so their actions can affect what other visitors see. Its profile and account settings are protected; event creation and participation remain available. Do not add personal details to the shared account's public event content.

3. Events and registrations

You can create, edit and cancel test events and register for available places, subject to the dates, capacity limits and permissions shown in the application. Event listings and the contact email supplied by their organiser are public. Organisers can see confirmed participants' usernames and account emails, including when using the shared demo account.

Account verification and recovery use email. The application does not currently send automatic email notifications for event registration, changes or cancellation; check your events in the application. Prices shown on listings are demonstration information. Tabula Belli does not take payments, sell tickets or process refunds.

  • Do not submit unlawful, abusive, deceptive or spam content, or personal information about other people without permission.
  • Do not access accounts or records you are not authorised to use, bypass security controls or deliberately disrupt the service.
  • Contact the developer if you encounter a problem or suspect misuse.

Content or access may be removed or restricted where necessary to address misuse.

5. Demo refreshes and deleting your account

The demo is refreshed every two calendar months. Visitor accounts, events and registrations are removed and the initial dataset is rebuilt with fresh event dates. Temporary maintenance may occur during this process. Visitor data is not intended to be kept permanently.

Your account area also provides a deletion flow. It cancels future organised events and pending participation while retaining historical records without your account links. Deletion is blocked while an event you organise is in progress. The shared demo account and administrative accounts are protected from this self-service flow. See the Privacy Policy for the treatment of contact messages and other data.
