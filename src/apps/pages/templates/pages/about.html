About Tabula Belli

Tabula Belli is a web application for tabletop communities. It brings together wargames, role-playing games and trading card games events in a single place, so players and organisers do not have to jump between scattered groups and outdated websites to know what is happening.

Why Tabula Belli exists

In most cities, tabletop events are announced across many different channels: WhatsApp groups, Discord servers, Facebook communities…

Information is easy to miss, hard to search and difficult to compare.

Tabula Belli focuses on one clear goal: give players and organisers a modern, central place to publish and discover events, with real locations, real games and clear rules for registrations.

TTRPG sessions: one-shots, campaigns and narrative play.

Who Tabula Belli is for

  • Local game stores and clubs that run regular tournaments, leagues, narrative campaigns or demo days and want a simple way to show everything they offer.
  • Players who want to quickly see what is scheduled near them, filter by game or format and register without sending dozens of messages back and forth.
  • Independent organisers who need a tool to publish events, control capacity and keep track of registrations.

Trading Card Game events: drafts, constructed and casual play.

What Tabula Belli does today

The current version of Tabula Belli offers a focused set of features that cover the basics of event discovery and management:

  • Public events catalogue with filters by game, category (TCG, TTRPG, wargame), type (casual or tournament), status and location (country, region, city).
  • Player accounts with registration, login and a personal "My Events" area to see upcoming events, registrations and events organised by the user.
  • Event management for organisers: create, edit and cancel events with clear rules about dates, capacity and visibility.
  • European location catalogue: 43 countries with their regions and thousands of cities to help you find the right place to play.
  • Game catalogue: over 120 games across TCGs, TTRPGs and wargames. Missing your favourite game? Contact us.

Wargames and miniatures: tournaments and friendly battles.

More time playing, less time planning

Whether you are looking for a casual game night or organising a tournament, Tabula Belli keeps the essentials together: the game, the place, the date and the available spots. Discover your next event, join a table or bring your own community together.

Your personal My Events area gives you one place to manage the events you organise and the ones you have joined, with clear registration windows and capacity limits.

Well, actually…

This public site is an interactive portfolio demo. The reference locations come from GeoNames and the games are real; the initial events and player profiles are fictional. Visitors can explore the shared demo account or create their own account with an email they can verify. Listings and registrations are for testing, not real bookings. Visitor activity is cleared during the demo refresh every two months. See our Privacy Policy for details.
