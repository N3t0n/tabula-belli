Privacy Policy

Last updated: 8 October 2026

1. About this policy

Tabula Belli is an interactive portfolio project operated by the n3t0n. This policy explains how personal data is used on the published demo. For privacy questions or requests, use the Contact page.

The initial events and player profiles are fictional. Visitors may also create accounts and test activity. These accounts and records are real data even though the events are demonstrations.

2. Data we use and why

  • Account details: your username, email address and password hash, used to create your account, sign you in and support email verification and account recovery. A working email address is required for verification.
  • Optional profile details: first and last name, country, region, city and favourite games, if you choose to add them. You can edit or remove these details from your profile; real names are not required.
  • Event and registration details: the listings you create, event contact email, dates, location, game, capacity and your registration status, used to display events and manage participation.
  • Contact messages: the email address, subject and message you send through the Contact form, used to read and respond to your enquiry.
  • Technical data: session and security information, request details and operational logs used to run the website, diagnose faults and prevent misuse. Hosting and other service providers may process IP addresses and browser information when receiving requests.

Account and event data is processed to provide the features you request. Contact handling, site maintenance and security are based on the legitimate interests of operating the project and responding to enquiries. Where a legal obligation applies, information may also be processed to meet that obligation. The project does not send marketing newsletters.

3. Who can see your information

  • Public event information: event listings, the organiser's username and the contact email supplied for the event are public. That contact email can be different from the email used for your account.
  • Participant information: the event organiser can see confirmed participants' usernames and account emails. The participant list is not public. The same access applies when an event is organised through the shared demo account.
  • Your profile: your optional profile details and favourite games are shown in your own account area, rather than on a public player profile.
  • Administration: the project operator can access stored records where needed to maintain the application or handle a request. Passwords are stored as hashes, not as readable passwords.

Do not include personal information about other people in public event descriptions or addresses.

4. Services used by the website

  • Northflank: hosts the application and its PostgreSQL database. The deployment region currently used is London, United Kingdom.
  • Resend: delivers transactional account emails, such as verification and password recovery messages. See Resend's Privacy Policy.
  • Formspree: receives contact submissions directly from your browser and forwards them to the project operator's inbox. The message may be retained in Formspree and in that inbox. See Formspree's Privacy Policy.
  • Maps: event pages with a map load Leaflet resources from UNPKG and map tiles from OpenStreetMap. These services receive technical connection data when your browser loads their resources. See OpenStreetMap's Privacy Policy. The optional Google Maps link opens an external service only when you select it.

Some providers may process information outside the EU or EEA. Their processing locations, safeguards and retention practices are described in their service documentation and privacy policies. The map uses the event city's coordinates; the application does not request your device's GPS location. GeoNames supplies the bundled location catalogue and is not queried as you browse.

5. Cookies and browser storage

The application uses session cookies to keep you signed in and CSRF cookies to protect forms. Selecting Remember me allows the login session to persist beyond closing the browser. Your light or dark theme preference is saved in your browser's local storage.

Tabula Belli does not include advertising cookies, tracking pixels or an analytics service. External services have their own privacy practices. Blocking required cookies may prevent login or form actions; you can clear cookies and the saved theme through your browser settings.

6. Retention, account deletion and demo refreshes

The published demo is refreshed every two calendar months. Visitor accounts, events and registrations are removed and replaced with fresh demonstration data. The reference location catalogue is retained. Do not use this demo as permanent storage or to arrange real bookings.

You can request account deletion through your account area before a refresh. Deletion cancels your upcoming organised events and pending participation, removes your account and event contact emails, and keeps historical event and participation records without links to your account. If an event you organise is in progress, this self-service deletion is blocked until it ends. The shared demo account cannot be deleted through the application.

Deletion does not automatically remove personal information you wrote into event titles, descriptions or addresses. Contact us if such text needs to be removed. Messages in Formspree and the operator's inbox are kept for handling and following up enquiries, separately from demo data. Demo refreshes and account deletion do not automatically erase those messages or providers' operational logs. You can contact us to request deletion of information that is no longer needed.

7. Security and your choices

The application uses HTTPS on the published site, password hashing, access controls and protections against cross-site request forgery. No online service can guarantee absolute security. Keep your personal account credentials private; the deliberately shared demo credentials are an exception.

Depending on applicable data protection law, you may request access, correction, deletion, restriction or portability of your personal data, or object to processing based on legitimate interests. You can edit your profile and use account deletion where available. For other requests, use the Contact page. We may need to verify that a request concerns your own data.

You may also raise a complaint with your competent data protection authority. This policy does not limit your statutory rights.

8. Changes and contact

If the way the project uses data changes, this policy and its update date will be revised. You can ask questions about the current policy through the Contact page.
